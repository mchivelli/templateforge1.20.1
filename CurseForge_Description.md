# The Warborn Origins

An Origins addon for Minecraft Forge 1.20.1 that adds 14 playable races, each with unique powers, strengths, and weaknesses. Built as an addon for the Origins mod.

---

## Origins

### Arachnae
![Arachnae](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/Arachnae.png)

*Tall spider-folk who scale walls and trap enemies in webs. Spiders see them as kin, but they are highly vulnerable to the Bane of Arthropods enchantment.*

* **Arachnid Stature:** 1.2x height, 0.95x width via Pehkui
* **Wall Climber:** Climb walls by walking into them. Hold sneak to cling
* **Arthropod Bane:** +50% damage taken from Bane of Arthropods enchanted weapons
* **Spider Friend:** Spiders and Cave Spiders are passive towards you
* **Master of Webs:** Active Ability: Hitting enemies places cobwebs at their position (8s cooldown)
* **Web Walker:** Not slowed by cobwebs
* **Race Attribute:** Carnivore

---

### Dark Dwarf
![Dark Dwarf](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/Dark_Dwarf.png)

*Cursed dwarves bound to soul fire. Their tainted blood nullifies potions and makes Smite devastating. They mend near soul light, and their chains of death pierce all who stand in their way.*

* **Dark Stature:** 0.85x height, 1.05x width via Pehkui
* **Soul Mending:** Regeneration I when within 8 blocks of soul light sources
* **Cursed:** +75% damage from Smite-enchanted weapons
* **Potion Immunity:** Immune to all potion effects (positive and negative)
* **Chains of Death:** Active Ability: Wither II + 4 magic damage to nearby enemies. Hold Shift for explosive variant. 20s cooldown
* **Race Attribute:** Mine Faster (+30% mining speed)

---

### Moon Elf (Dark Elf)
![Moon Elf](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/Dark_Elf.png)

*Children of the night, Moon Elves are deadly assassins who meld with shadows. Their lunar-touched skin burns in water, but under moonlight they become truly invisible.*

* **Lunar Stature:** 1.15x height, 0.9x width via Pehkui
* **Nimble:** 15% chance to dodge attacks, +10% movement speed
* **Moonlit Veil:** Invisibility while sneaking at night (uses stealth meter)
* **Night Strike:** 2x bonus damage when attacking from stealth at night
* **Assassin's Focus:** -30% damage dealt when stealth meter is not full
* **Hydrophobia:** 1 damage per second in water or rain (Aqua Affinity protects)
* **Fragile:** -6 max health, +15% physical damage taken
* **Ethereal:** Faint blue lunar glow on player model
* **Race Attribute:** Vegetarian

---

### Goblin
![Goblin](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/Goblin.png)

*Small and cunning, Goblins are obsessed with gold and treasure. Their strong stomachs and nimble fingers make them excellent scavengers, though villagers charge them dearly.*

* **Goblin Stature:** 0.75x height, 0.85x width via Pehkui
* **Gold Affinity:** Gold weapons/armor gain substantial stats (equivalent to Netherite)
* **Haggler:** Villagers charge significantly higher prices
* **Iron Stomach:** Immune to Hunger and Poison effects
* **Lucky Fingers:** Passive Luck I. Active Ability: Luck III burst for 15s (30s cooldown)
* **Skinny:** -4 max health
* **Race Attribute:** Carnivore

---

### High Elf
![High Elf](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/High_Elf.png)

*Masters of the arcane arts, High Elves trade physical resilience for magical prowess. Their innate talent makes them formidable spellcasters, but heavy armor encumbers their graceful forms.*

* **Arcane Stature:** 1.15x height, 0.9x width via Pehkui
* **Fragile:** -4 max health, +20% physical damage taken
* **Arcane Mastery:** +20% magic resistance, +25% magic damage dealt
* **Encumbered:** -10% movement speed per heavy armor piece (Feather Falling negates)
* **Arcane Surge:** Active Ability: Regeneration II and Resistance I. Requires magical catalyst (20s cooldown)
* **Ethereal:** Faint purple-blue magical shimmer on player model
* **Race Attribute:** Vegetarian

### Human
![Human](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/Human.png)

*Classic Minecraft adventurers with no special advantages or disadvantages.*

* **None:** Baseline vanilla gameplay

### Mountain Dwarf
![Mountain Dwarf](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/Mountain_Dwarf.png)

*Stout miners born deep under the mountains. Their hardy constitution grants extra health and mining fortune, but they sink in water and burn in sunlight. A swig of dwarven brew heals their wounds.*

* **Mountain Stature:** 0.8x height, 1.1x width via Pehkui
* **Hardy:** +6 max health
* **Fortune Born:** Permanent Luck II (affects ore drops and loot)
* **Dense Body:** Cannot swim, sinks in water, Slowness while submerged
* **Sun Sensitivity:** 1 damage every 2s in direct sunlight (helmet protects)
* **Dwarven Brew:** Active Ability: Instant Health II, Absorption I, Saturation (25s cooldown)
* **Race Attribute:** Mine Faster (+30% mining speed)

---

### Revenant (Necromancer)
![Revenant](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/Necromancer.png)

*Betrayers who forged Mortanis's prison, cursed to undeath. Denied Luminara's gifts, they cannot harvest crops and are burned by Smite. Yet, the dead recognize them as kin, and they sustain themselves by draining essence from the living.*

* **Undead Nature:** Counted as undead (undead mobs are non-hostile)
* **Essence Extraction:** On hit, deals bonus magic damage to living targets and grants Absorption
* **Black Thumb:** Cannot harvest crops
* **Necrotic Resilience:** Immune to Poison and Wither
* **Consecrated Weakness:** +60% damage from Smite-enchanted weapons
* **Starved Husk:** +100% starvation damage
* **Disturbed:** Cannot sleep (can still set spawn point)
* **Revenance:** Active Ability: Summon a zombie and skeleton servant (35s cooldown)

---

### Orc
![Orc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/Orc.png)

*Created by goblins as brutish slaves, Orcs cast off their chains. Towering and powerful, they embody raw fury, but their goblin blood curses them with an insatiable hunger and restless anger that drives them ever toward conflict.*

* **Orc Stature:** 1.25x height, 1.15x width via Pehkui
* **Battering Ram:** +100% knockback and +30% attack damage while sprinting
* **Ravenous:** Hunger depletes 50% faster
* **Powerful Legs:** +75% jump/step height
* **Dashing Supremacy:** Active Ability: Charge forward with Speed III, Resistance I, dealing 4 AoE damage and Slowness (15s cooldown)
* **Tough Skin:** +4 natural armor, -10% movement speed
* **Race Attribute:** Carnivore

---

### Piglin Kind
![Piglin Kind](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/Piglin_Kind.png)

*Cursed remnants of mortal greed punished by Mortanis. Twisted into swine-like bodies, Piglin Kind crave gold. Bound to the Nether, these Warborn embrace their curse, turning survival into a weapon of dominion.*

* **Piglin Stature:** 1.05x height, 1.05x width via Pehkui
* **Fire Resistant:** Permanent Fire Resistance
* **Fire Hunger:** Standing in fire or lava restores hunger
* **Hydrophobia:** 1 damage every 1.5s in water or rain (Aqua Affinity protects)
* **Nether Strength:** Nether: +25% attack damage, +10% speed. Overworld: -15% attack damage, -10% speed
* **Unholy Deal:** Active Ability: Sacrifice 4 health for Strength II and Speed I (20s cooldown)
* **Race Attribute:** Night Vision

---

### Plague Victim
![Plague Victim](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/Plague_Person.png)

*Rotting carriers of a cruel affliction. Undead leave them alone and poison cannot harm them, but villagers shun them and sleep is impossible.*

* **Undead Nature:** Counted as undead
* **Plague Immunity:** Immune to Poison
* **Contagious:** Villagers refuse to trade
* **Insomniac:** Cannot sleep (can still set spawn point)
* **Poison Cloud:** Active Ability: AoE poison + magic damage around the player (25s cooldown)

---

### Siren
![Siren](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/Siren.png)

*Enchanting water-dwellers who thrive beneath the waves. Sirens gain Dolphin's Grace and regeneration in water, but weaken on land. Their mesmerizing song pacifies hostile creatures.*

* **Siren Stature:** 0.9x width via Pehkui
* **Aquatic Grace:** In water: Dolphin's Grace, Regeneration I, Water Breathing
* **Land Weakness:** On land: -15% movement speed, -20% attack damage
* **Siren's Charm:** Permanent Hero of the Village I
* **Siren's Song:** Active Ability: Pacify hostile mobs within 10 blocks (Slowness V + Weakness III for 8s). 30s cooldown
* **Race Attribute:** Night Vision

---

### Wild Dwarf
![Wild Dwarf](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/Wild_Dwarf.png)

*Wandering survivors of the wilds. They haggle better prices but are vulnerable to explosions. At night they become faster but weaker - a shield negates this weakness at the cost of speed. Their energy shockwave pushes enemies back.*

* **Wild Stature:** 0.85x height via Pehkui
* **Born Trader:** Permanent Hero of the Village I
* **Blast Vulnerable:** +20% explosion damage taken
* **Nocturnal Surge:** Night: +20% speed and Weakness I. Shield negates weakness but removes speed bonus
* **Energy Shockwave:** Active Ability: Push all enemies within 5 blocks back, 3 damage + Slowness II (18s cooldown)
* **Race Attribute:** Mine Faster (+30% mining speed)

---

### Wood Elf
![Wood Elf](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mchivelli/Warborn-Origins/main/warbornoriginimages/Wood_Elf.png)

*Born of ancient forests, Wood Elves are graceful hunters. They move silently through tall grass, vanishing from sight. Flowers grant them passive gifts, and their nimble frames absorb falls easily - though fire remains their greatest fear.*

* **Elven Stature:** 1.2x height via Pehkui
* **Nature's Veil:** Invisibility and night vision while standing in vegetation
* **Flammable:** +50% fire, lava, and flame damage taken
* **Light on Feet:** -50% fall damage
* **Flower Affinity:** Carry 3+ of a flower type for passive effects (max 2 simultaneously)
* **Skinny:** Slender elven physique
* **Race Attribute:** Vegetarian

---

## Shared Attributes Note

* **Vegetarian:** Eating meat causes Nausea, Poison, and Hunger.
* **Carnivore:** Eating plant-based food causes Nausea, Weakness, and Hunger.
* **Mine Faster:** +30% mining speed.
* **Night Vision:** Permanent Night Vision.
